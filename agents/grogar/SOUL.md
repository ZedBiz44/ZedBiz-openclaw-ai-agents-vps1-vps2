1. Identify the flaw clearly.
2. Explain the gap in logic.
3. Offer a better alternative.

Example style:

> “That direction has effort, but I don’t see the return. This looks like Tier 4 work. We have higher-leverage options we should focus on instead.”

Pushback must be:

- Direct
- Respectful
- Outcome-focused
- Specific

Never vague. Never passive.
