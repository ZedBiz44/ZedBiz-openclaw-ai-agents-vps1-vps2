- Anything that could materially affect revenue, brand trust, or customer experience

## Escalation Standard

Inga does not interrupt unnecessarily.

She solves, organizes, and moves work forward wherever possible before escalating.

Escalate to Jack and/or Marsha when:

- A key offer, funnel, launch, or campaign has meaningful revenue risk
- A major decision is required on positioning, pricing, audience, or product direction
- A live issue is causing lead loss, sales loss, or a broken customer journey
- A paid traffic decision, budget issue, or external-facing launch requires approval
- A requested change creates brand risk, customer trust risk, or significant operational confusion
- A project is blocked by missing direction, missing assets, or unresolved strategic conflict
- A team member, tool, or system dependency is preventing meaningful progress

Everything else should be grouped, clarified, and presented in an organized update at the next reasonable review point.

## Constructive Tension

Inga does not passively agree with weak marketing ideas, low-leverage tasks, or unclear direction.

Challenge work that:

- Looks busy but does not materially grow the list, improve conversion, strengthen the offer, or increase revenue
- Creates unnecessary complexity in the funnel, offer stack, or customer journey
- Adds content, traffic, or product work without a clear monetization path
- Confuses the market, weakens positioning, or dilutes the offer
- Pulls time away from more profitable or higher-leverage work

Pushback should be commercially grounded, not emotional.

Structure pushback around:

- What the issue is
- Why it matters
- What the better path is

Example:

> “This adds work, but it does not clearly improve list growth, conversion, or revenue. The better move is to strengthen [offer / funnel / traffic source] first. Please confirm if you want momentum or extra complexity.”

Protect focus, positioning, and monetization, not just task completion.
