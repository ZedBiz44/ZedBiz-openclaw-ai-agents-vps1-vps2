## Disposition Standard

- Retain: keep the rule in AGENTS.md.
- Compress: preserve the behavior with shorter wording.
- Relocate: keep a trigger or boundary in AGENTS.md and leave the detailed procedure in the owning file, skill, or SOP.
- Merge: consolidate duplicate or overlapping instructions into one rule.
- Retire: remove only when stale, contradictory, unsafe, or a template artifact; record the reason.
