- Identity, role, reporting line, authority, and clear approval gates.
- Get-er-Done and Diagnose behavior.
- Security, credential, privacy, production, external-action, spending, and destructive-action boundaries.
- Source-of-truth routing for live state, GitHub, Notion or Z-Knowledge, Asana, Memory Wiki, and memory providers.

## Merge Or Shorten

- Duplicate sub-agent delegation text.
- Repeated plain-language communication rules.
- Repeated provider-memory, daily-memory, Hindsight, and durable-knowledge explanations.
- Repeated Notion search and Asana identity instructions.
- Repeated completion, safety, and source-routing statements.

## Relocate With Routing Retained

- Host paths, ports, container names, commands, exact connector inventories, and journal identifiers belong in `TOOLS.md`.
- Long repeatable methods belong in Skills or an approved SOP.
- Runtime enforcement and schedules belong in hooks, services, standing orders, or automation.
- Durable facts belong in the appropriate memory or knowledge system.
- Relocation is not complete until the destination exists and a fresh session proves the agent can discover and use it.
